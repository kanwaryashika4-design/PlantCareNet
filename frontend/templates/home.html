Workflow

How Plant Care Net Works

A three-stage AI pipeline from raw input to actionable, explainable output

01
📥

Input Collection

Farmer uploads a tomato leaf photograph. Researcher enters 6 soil and environmental parameters. Both paths feed their respective AI engines automatically.

→
02
🧠

AI Processing

MobileNetV2 CNN classifies disease type with 93.88% accuracy. OpenCV HSV pixel masking quantifies infected leaf area percentage. Random Forest predicts nutrient deficiency class.

→
03
📊

Explainable Report

Complete diagnostic output — disease name, confidence score, severity level, Grad-CAM heatmap showing AI focus regions, SHAP feature importance chart, and a priority treatment plan.

Capabilities

What Makes Plant Care Net Different

Unlike systems that only identify disease type, this platform performs multilevel crop health analysis

🔍
Multilevel Crop Analysis

Disease classification, infection severity quantification, and nutrient deficiency analysis combined — not just disease type identification alone.

📐
Infection Severity Quantification

HSV-based pixel masking calculates the exact percentage of leaf area showing disease symptoms — mild (0–20%), moderate (21–50%), or severe (51–100%).

🧠
Grad-CAM Explainability

Visual heatmap overlay shows exactly which leaf regions the AI focused on when making the disease diagnosis — transparent and trustworthy predictions.

📊
SHAP Feature Importance

Bar chart shows which of the 6 soil and environmental parameters most influenced the nutrient deficiency classification — essential for research analysis.

🛡️
Robust Image Validation

Multi-layer validation using confidence threshold, green pixel ratio, color variance, edge density, and dominant color analysis rejects charts, graphs, and non-leaf images.

⚡
Real-Time Inference

Both AI models serve predictions in real time — disease diagnosis with Grad-CAM heatmap generation completes in under 5 seconds on standard hardware.
