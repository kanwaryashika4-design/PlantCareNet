Bacterial Spot

Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria
High Risk
Visual Symptoms

Small, water-soaked spots (1–3 mm) on leaves that turn dark brown to black with yellow halos. Fruit develops raised, scabby spots.

Optimal Development Conditions

High humidity, Warm temps (24–30°C), Wind-driven rain, Infected seeds

🧪 Treatment Protocol

Apply copper-based bactericide (Copper Oxychloride 50% WP). Remove infected debris. Avoid overhead irrigation.

Early Blight

Alternaria solani
Moderate Risk
Visual Symptoms

Circular to irregular dark brown lesions with concentric rings forming a "target board" pattern, starting on older lower leaves.

Optimal Development Conditions

Warm temps (24–29°C), High humidity, Nitrogen deficiency, Stressed plants

🧪 Treatment Protocol

Apply chlorothalonil or mancozeb fungicide. Improve plant nutrition — nitrogen-deficient plants are highly susceptible.

Late Blight

Phytophthora infestans
Critical Risk
Visual Symptoms

Large, irregular, water-soaked lesions that rapidly turn dark brown to black. White fuzzy sporulation visible on leaf undersides.

Optimal Development Conditions

Cool temps (10–25°C), Very high humidity, Prolonged leaf wetness

🧪 Treatment Protocol

Apply copper-based fungicide immediately. Remove and destroy all infected plant material — do not compost.
